Latency

The DSP adds no buffering of its own. Every stage is causal, and an impulse comes out on the same sample it went in. On a Daisy Seed with 4-sample blocks, the only delay is the converters plus about 0.2 ms of buffering. The browser adds 10–40 ms, so the live mic here is for auditioning the sound, not for judging feel.

Feedback

The drive adds up to 30 dB of gain, so room noise and wedge spill get louder too. That loop is what makes feedback. Set Chop so the gate LED lights only while you sing. Keep Lo cut up, use a dynamic cardioid mic, and use in-ears instead of a wedge where you can.
