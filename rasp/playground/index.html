Momentary, on while held (or hold M / T / S / G / Z / F / A). Mega: hard-clipped mid burst. Throw: sends the voice to a long delay that rings out after you let go. Stutter: repeats the slice that starts when you press, on the tempo grid. Glitch: the same, with repeats that roll, reverse, drop an octave or cut out; above 50% amount it crashes like a computer (crush, ring mod, pitch jumps, skips, buzz). Tape stop: the voice slows to a stop. Freeze: the moment before the press sustains as a drone. Gate: chops the voice on the grid.

Menu

On the hardware: level faders and three trim pots. Set the bleed reducer at soundcheck: with the band playing and you silent, turn it up until the bleed LED stays lit.

Latency

The DSP adds no buffering of its own. Nothing looks ahead. The only delay is about 0.15 ms of filter phase delay in the drive path, the same kind an analog tone control has. On a Daisy Seed with 4-sample blocks, the only delay is the converters plus about 0.2 ms of buffering. The browser adds 10–40 ms, so the live mic here is for auditioning the sound, not for judging feel.

Browser audio latency: not started.

Feedback

A produced chain is heavily compressed, so drums, amps and wedges in your mic get louder too. That causes mud and feedback. The bleed reducer turns them back down between phrases. In testing it restored 24.8 of 28.4 dB of voice-to-bleed separation. Use the Live stage preset, keep Lo cut up, use a dynamic cardioid mic, and use in-ears rather than a wedge where you can.
